Questions, answered before they scuttle off

Is Pinch really free?

Yes. Up to 3 people and 2 boards, forever, no credit card. We make money when teams grow into Pro, not by holding your tasks hostage.

Can I import from Trello, Asana, or a spreadsheet?

Drop in a CSV or a Trello JSON export and Pinch maps lists to columns automatically. Asana import is in beta — ask and we'll switch it on.

Why only three columns?

Because every extra column is a place for work to hide. Three columns keep things honest: not started, started, finished. You can still use labels for nuance.

What happens if I cancel?

Your team drops back to Free and keeps every card. Export everything to CSV whenever you like — no exit interview, no guilt trip.

Is the crab a real employee?

He's on the org chart as “Head of Grip”. He does not attend meetings, which is why he's so productive.
